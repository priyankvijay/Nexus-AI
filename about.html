Under the Surface

How NEXUS Thinks

Six stages from your input to a perfectly crafted response.

⌨️
Input Tokenization
Your message is split into semantic tokens — the atomic units of meaning — ready for neural processing.
🔍
Context Encoding
Self-attention layers weigh every token against every other, building a rich representation of meaning and intent.
🧮
Deep Inference
96 transformer layers refine the representation, drawing on 175B learned parameters from training on trillions of tokens.
🎯
Intent Classification
A dedicated classifier identifies the goal behind your message — question, command, creative request, or emotional support.
📝
Response Generation
Autoregressive decoding produces the optimal response token-by-token, balancing fluency, accuracy, and tone.
✅
Safety & Quality Filter
A final review layer checks for accuracy, helpfulness, and policy compliance before delivering your answer.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NEXUS AI free to use? +
Yes! NEXUS AI offers a generous free tier with unlimited basic conversations. Pro and Enterprise plans unlock advanced features, higher rate limits, priority access, and API integrations.
How does NEXUS protect my privacy? +
We use AES-256 encryption for all data in transit and at rest. We never sell personal data to third parties. Users can delete their conversation history at any time from the settings panel. Our infrastructure is SOC 2 Type II certified.
What languages does NEXUS support? +
NEXUS currently supports 20+ languages including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Portuguese, and more. We add new languages quarterly based on user demand and community voting.
Can NEXUS write code? +
Absolutely! NEXUS excels at programming tasks — generating code, debugging, explaining algorithms, reviewing pull requests, and teaching programming concepts across JavaScript, Python, Rust, Go, Java, C++, and many other languages.
Is NEXUS AI always online? +
We maintain 99.9% uptime guaranteed by SLA. Our distributed infrastructure across 130+ global PoPs ensures that even if one region experiences issues, traffic automatically reroutes. Planned maintenance is announced 72 hours in advance.
How accurate is NEXUS AI? +
NEXUS achieves 99.7% accuracy on standardized NLP benchmarks. For factual questions, we provide source citations where possible. Like all AI systems, NEXUS may occasionally produce errors — always verify critical information from authoritative sources.
Can I use NEXUS for my business? +
Yes! Our Enterprise plan includes API access, custom model fine-tuning, white-label options, SLA guarantees, dedicated support, and SSO integration. Contact our sales team for custom pricing based on your organization's scale and needs.
